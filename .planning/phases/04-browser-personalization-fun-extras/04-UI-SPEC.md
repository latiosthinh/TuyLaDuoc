# Phase 4 — UI Design Contract

## Visual Presentation & Extras Contract
- **Hòm của tôi Dialog:** Slide-over or modal with custom dish input form (name, price, category), dish chips with delete icon, and empty guidance.
- **Spin Source Toggle:** Switch toggle above filters: "Trạm tiếp tế" vs "Hòm của tôi ({N})".
- **Quẻ trưa (/que-trua):** Center mystical bamboo/golden slip with tap-to-draw animation, auspicious quote, and suggested lucky dish.
- **Duyên vị (/duyen-vi):** Split-column pairing card highlighting two dishes that match flavors.
- **Khui vị (/khui-vi):** Mystery present box with unbox button and sparkle reveal.
- **Legal Pages (/privacy, /terms):** Prose article layout with clean typography.
